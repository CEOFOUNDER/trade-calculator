How the calculation works

Mid-price = (bid + ask) / 2. Spread = ask − bid. Base price move D = mid-price × your entered percentage / 100, before spread. Assumes the spread stays constant as the market moves.

Long enters at ask: SL (bid) = current bid − D; TP (bid) = current bid + D × RRR. Short enters at bid: SL (ask) = current ask + D; TP (ask) = current ask − D × RRR.

For either direction, price risk per unit = D + spread; price reward per unit = D × RRR − spread. The resulting reward-to-risk ratio after spread is reward / risk, before fees and slippage. A target that cannot cover the current spread is rejected.

Example: 105.51 / 106.32 gives mid-price 105.915 and base move 1.05915. At base price move 1% and RRR 1: long SL 104.45085 / TP 106.56915; short SL 107.37915 / TP 105.26085. Price risk is 1.86915 and reward is 0.24915 per unit after spread.

Calculator only; no orders are sent. Assumes positive prices and bid/ask execution. Match your broker’s trigger rules and tick size before entering orders. Display rounding, slippage, fees, funding and changing spreads can affect actual results. Shorting availability depends on the instrument and broker.
