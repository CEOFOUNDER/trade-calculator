How the calculation works

Midpoint = (bid + ask) / 2. Spread = ask − bid. The stop-loss percentage defines planned price risk per unit, using the midpoint as the percentage base. Take-profit distance = stop-loss distance × RRR. RRR 1 means equal planned reward and risk.

Long SL = ask − midpoint × SL%. Long TP = ask + midpoint × SL% × RRR. Short SL = bid + midpoint × SL%. Short TP = bid − midpoint × SL% × RRR. These are closing-side quote levels, so the entry spread is already included; do not add it again.

With 105.51 / 106.32 and 1% stop distance, RRR 1: midpoint 105.915; long SL 105.26085, TP 107.37915; short SL 106.56915, TP 104.45085.

Calculator only; no orders are sent. Assumes positive prices and bid/ask execution. Match your broker’s trigger rules and tick size before entering orders.
